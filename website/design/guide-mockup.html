Move your Claude connectors to pMCPg

Claude keeps the connectors you add in Anthropic's cloud, under your Claude account. pMCPg can't read them, so you bring each one over once. It takes about a minute per connector.

Stored in the cloudConnectors live in your Claude account, not in a file on your computer.
No export buttonClaude has no way to export them, so no app can import them for you.
Once, then doneAfter the move, every AI app on your computer uses them through pMCPg.

Way 1: let Claude list them Fastest

Claude writes the list of your connectors. You paste it into pMCPg.

1

Copy the message from pMCPg

In pMCPg, open Import and choose Ask your chatbot to list them. Click Copy to clipboard.

pMCPg
Bring over your Claude or ChatGPT connectors

Claude and ChatGPT keep the connectors you add on their websites in their own cloud, not on this computer.

Send it to Claude

Start a new chat in Claude with your connectors turned on. Paste the message and send it. Claude answers with a list.

Paste the answer into pMCPg

Paste the whole answer under 2. Paste the answer here and choose Find servers. Tick the ones to add. Then sign in to each one, once.

Only names and web addresses are read from the answer. A row marked URL needed, like Research MCP above, means Claude didn't know the address: get it with Way 2.

What stays in Claude

Rows marked Built-in integration · By Anthropic, like GitHub in step 2 of Way 2, are part of Claude. They have no Connector URL, so they can't move. Keep them on in Claude.
