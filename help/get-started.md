**On a Mac**, macOS may ask whether pmcpg can use its keychain the first time it starts. Enter your Mac password and choose **Always Allow**: see [The Mac keychain prompt](help:keychain-prompt).

## 1. Add a server

A server gives your AI tools. Add one from [Servers](/servers/?add=1) by pasting its web address, or bring in the ones you already use from [Import](/import/). See [Add a server by URL](help:add-server-by-url) and [Import from your AI apps](help:import-from-ai-apps).

## 2. Sign in

Online services usually ask you to sign in once in your browser, or to paste an API key. pMCPg keeps the sign-in fresh after that. [Accounts](/accounts/) shows which ones still need you.

## 3. Connect your AI app

Open [Connect your AI app](/connect/) and pick your app. pMCPg shows what it will change, saves a backup, and makes the change for you. Then restart the app.

Guides: [Claude Desktop](help:connect-claude-desktop), [Claude Code](help:connect-claude-code), [LM Studio](help:connect-lm-studio), [Cursor and VS Code](help:connect-cursor-vscode).

## 4. Set a password

Optional on your own computer, and a good idea on a shared one. See [Set a password for this page](help:ui-password).

## Then

- Make a profile per purpose or per app: [Profiles](help:profiles).
- Pick how much personal data to hide: [Pick a privacy level](help:privacy-levels).
